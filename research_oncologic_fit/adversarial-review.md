# Adversarial review

11 October 2026. Self-critique following independent clinical/governance/prototype strands.

1. **Are we equating a similar company with validation?** No. Oncologic establishes positioning overlap only. Its linked outcome evidence was inaccessible. Require study methods, deployment demonstrations and definitions before comparative efficacy or adoption claims.
2. **Could the proposed software make work worse?** Yes. Current broad revision invalidation and research-applicability requirements could generate unnecessary review. Extra data entry and unstaffed alerts can move burden to nurses/coordinators. Measure all roles, not just faster physician clicks.
3. **What is the strongest alternative explanation for the problem?** Capacity, staffing, affordability, data-sharing permission or a poorly integrated existing hospital system may dominate. A focused integration or process change may outperform a new application. The present research does not establish a unique market gap.
4. **What would reverse the build recommendation?** Clinicians cannot identify frequent relevant failures; current tools already solve them; safe use requires duplicate records; the service cannot own tasks/protocols; observed verification burden exceeds saved effort; or users will not return to the prototype. Those findings should narrow or stop expansion.

## Specific assumptions rejected or qualified

- One senior oncologist's judgment is not sufficient ground truth for every protocol. Peer appraisal, relevant disciplines, patient variation and documented exceptions remain necessary design inputs.
- More study citations do not automatically produce better patient decisions. Retrieval relevance, evidence strength, clinical applicability and review burden are different measures.
- A local outcome association cannot automatically establish treatment superiority. Separate descriptive quality metrics, hypotheses and causal research.
- More confirmations do not necessarily make software safer. Review scope must be understandable and specific enough to prevent fatigue.
- A fictional prototype passing software tests would not establish clinical accuracy; the current targeted test run also contains one stale failing setup.

## Triangulation limits

Public positioning: vendor-only, adequate to describe claims but not outcomes. Clinical shape: institutional guidance, professional standards and an adjacent randomized follow-up study; none evaluates this product. Protocol/research governance: institutional methods/policy, with local practice unobserved. Prototype behavior: direct source and UI evidence, but neither is a production trial. Local adoption and staffing outcomes remain underdetermined.
