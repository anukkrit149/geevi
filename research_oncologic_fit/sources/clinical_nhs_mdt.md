# NHS England: MDT streamlining

- URL: https://www.england.nhs.uk/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/multi-disciplinary-team-streamlining-guidance.pdf
- Title: Streamlining Multi-Disciplinary Team Meetings: Guidance for Cancer Alliances.
- Issued: August 2019; hosted January 2020. Retrieved: 11 October 2026.
- Type: Primary institutional operational guidance; pages 9–13 particularly relevant.
- Access: Read-only web retrieval fallback after gstack search/site access problems. Full document text retrieved, 20 pages.
- Quote: “with any treatment decision made by the responsible clinician and patient.”
- Claim: Protocol-based triage can focus full meetings on complex cases. All patients remain under MDT oversight. Eligibility needs complete tumour-specific information, specialist-reported pathology/imaging, relevant testing and attention to patient preferences. Doubt, changed information, or preferences inconsistent with a pathway trigger discussion. Case accuracy remains the referring clinician's responsibility. Standards require approval, defined criteria and review.
- Product implication: Human-confirmed readiness and exception routing are better initial automation targets than automatic treatment selection. Keep the MDT recommendation separate from the patient-agreed plan.
- Credibility: High for English NHS workflow expectations; does not prove effectiveness of a proposed product. Recency: Old but detailed process reference; do not claim current Indian policy. Bias/applicability: Public health-service guidance with streamlining objective and specific institutional structures. Local hospital workflow must be validated.
