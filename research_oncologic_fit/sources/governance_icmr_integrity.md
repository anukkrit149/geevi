## Verified claims
Section 5 calls for scientific advisory and ethics committee approval as applicable, conflict disclosure, competent qualified researchers, peer review and secure retention of raw data. It states trial registration is mandatory for clinical trials and desirable for other research. This source does not settle whether a particular local audit qualifies for exemption or consent waiver.

Quote (19 words): “Researcher/s should obtain approval of Scientific Advisory Committee (SAC) and EC as per norms and declare COI, if any.”

## Design inference
Research studies need a separate workspace with named investigator, study protocol, applicable institutional determination/approvals, data access scope, analysis specification and reproducible dataset snapshot. Clinical SOP publication and research authorization are different approval events. Do not claim every audit needs full committee review or every retrospective analysis is exempt.
