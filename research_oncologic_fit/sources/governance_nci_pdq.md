## Verified claims
Summaries are written/reviewed by subject matter experts, reference literature and communicate strength of evidence. They exist for professional and patient audiences.

Quote (9 words): “They are not clinical practice guidelines or treatment recommendations”.

## Implication and limitation
Evidence knowledge, an approved departmental protocol, and a patient-specific decision must remain distinct software objects. This is a proposed design inference, not an NCI software requirement. A retrieved evidence summary must not silently become a prescription or local SOP.
