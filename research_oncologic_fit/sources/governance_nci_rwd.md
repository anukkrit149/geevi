## Verified claims
The program prioritizes completeness, quality and collection of routine electronic-health-record data. It describes inconsistently stored data as a challenge, development of extraction/transformation guidance, data conformance and completeness checks, phenotype validation, and harmonization. Its 2023 supplement included language-model-assisted extraction from unstructured clinical/pathology reports and privacy-preserving collaborative research.

Quote (9 words): “Today, however, RWD in many EHRs is messy.”

## Design inference and limitation
Start the research capability with reliable care data, source provenance and quality checks; a polished cohort dashboard alone does not solve the data problem. NCI program existence is evidence of a recognized problem, not proof this local product will be adopted or improve patient outcomes.
