# Geevi oncologist demonstration research

Question: What information and evidence workflow would let an oncologist understand Geevi, investigate a patient-specific question, and turn reviewed evidence into departmental practice?

Output: cited briefing, implementation review and a traceable research-document pack. This is a workflow demonstration, not a clinical recommendation or efficacy validation.

1. Patient overview and clinical workflow: identify decision-relevant fields and gaps in the current patient screens; compare against primary oncology documentation standards.
2. Research documentation: find accessible primary AML evidence and structured trial records; document publication, question, population, findings, limitations, availability and reuse terms. Do not invent papers or copy protected full text.
3. Departmental research and governance: inspect research/protocol/exception/follow-up screens against the plan and identify barriers to a clinician-led demonstration.

Synthesis: root agent integrates source findings, replaces fabricated research fixtures with attributed evidence briefs, improves the patient-to-research-to-protocol journey, and verifies the deployed demo. Public evidence remains separate from fictional patient records. Patient source documents remain visibly synthetic.

Research route: general research fallback with delegated subtopics using web-research; all browsing through gstack browse. Limit each delegated topic to 3–5 targeted searches and primary sources.
