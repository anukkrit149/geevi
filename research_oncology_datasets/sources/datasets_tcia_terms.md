# TCIA data-use policy

Retrieved 11 October 2026 via gstack. Primary current policy; inspect collection-specific rows before reuse.

- https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/data-usage-policies-and-restrictions/
- Policy says most data support commercial, scientific and educational use under CC BY 3.0/4.0, but some collections differ. Attribution must include the dataset citation and DOI. Identification/contact of participants is prohibited. Mirroring or providing access through another tool has additional attribution/downstream-policy requirements.
- Quote: “The corresponding license associated with each dataset hosted on TCIA can be seen in the Data Access table”.
- Since April 2025, datasets requiring data-use agreements are managed through NCI CRDC/dbGaP. Open images do not make related controlled clinical data open.
- Product implication: preserve a manifest of exact collection version, file, licence, attribution and allowed use; no blanket TCIA-wide permissive flag. A data licence never establishes fitness for clinical care.
