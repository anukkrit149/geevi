## Verified content and relevance
The current **Myeloid and Mixed / Ambiguous Lineage Neoplasms** protocol is **1.1.0.2, September 2026**. It explicitly covers AML and samples involving marrow, blood and other anatomical sites. Its first page says it is recommended for clinical care but not required for accreditation. The inspected portion distinguishes the sampled site, blast measurements, marrow cellularity, integrated diagnosis, morphology, flow cytometry, conventional cytogenetics, FISH and molecular studies. It distinguishes studies that are pending, not performed or uninformative from negative findings. These categories demonstrate why a gene-boolean summary is incomplete; no treatment thresholds or classification rules are adopted here.

This is a **reporting template**, not a filled authentic patient report and not a training dataset. The older 2016 bone-marrow synoptic guideline is labelled inactive since March 2021 in the official search-returned page; do not substitute it for the current myeloid protocol or describe it as current guidance.
